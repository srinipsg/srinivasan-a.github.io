01 · ABOUT

AI research with an engineering mindset.

Dr. Srinivasan A. is an Associate Professor in the Department of Computer Science & Engineering at NIT Puducherry. His career includes academic teaching, software engineering and interdisciplinary research at CSIR-CMERI.

His research spans machine learning, computer vision, medical image analysis, surveillance, robotics, intelligent sensing and post-stroke rehabilitation.
